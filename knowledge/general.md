# General Pet Care Guidelines

## Consistency and routine
tags: general, routine, schedule, consistency, time
Pets do best with predictable daily routines. Feeding, walks, and play at roughly
the same times each day reduce stress and behavior problems. When care time is
limited, protect the essentials first — feeding, medication, and toileting — before
optional activities like extended play.

## Prioritizing when time is short
tags: general, priority, time, busy, schedule
On a busy day, health-critical tasks come first: feeding, any medication, and
toileting needs (walks for dogs, litter for cats). Enrichment and grooming can be
shortened or shifted to another day without harm. A short walk or quick play
session is far better than skipping it entirely.

## Medication
tags: general, medication, meds, health, schedule
Medication should be given at consistent times as directed, and never skipped
without veterinary advice. Set reminders so doses aren't missed, and pair them with
an existing routine like a meal to make them easier to remember.

## Avoiding scheduling conflicts
tags: general, conflict, schedule, overlap, time
Avoid stacking demanding activities at the same time, especially in multi-pet
households. Spacing tasks out gives each pet individual attention and keeps the day
manageable. If two tasks are booked at the same time, move one earlier or later.

## Signs to watch for
tags: general, health, warning, vet, illness
Contact a vet if a pet shows sudden changes in appetite, energy, toileting, or
weight, or signs of pain. Early attention to these changes usually leads to better
outcomes.

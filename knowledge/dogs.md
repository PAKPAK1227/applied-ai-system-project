# Dog Care Guidelines

## Walking and exercise
tags: dog, walk, exercise, puppy, energy
Most adult dogs need 30-60 minutes of walking per day, ideally split into at
least two shorter walks rather than one long one. High-energy breeds (retrievers,
collies, shepherds) trend toward the upper end or beyond. Puppies need shorter,
more frequent walks — a rough guide is about 5 minutes per month of age, twice a
day, to protect developing joints.

## Feeding
tags: dog, feeding, food, meals, puppy
Adult dogs are usually fed twice a day on a consistent schedule. Puppies under six
months typically need three to four smaller meals a day. Keep feeding times
consistent and avoid a single large meal, which can raise the risk of bloat in
deep-chested breeds. Always provide fresh water.

## Grooming
tags: dog, grooming, brushing, coat, bath
Grooming frequency depends on coat type. Short-coated dogs need brushing about
once a week; long or double-coated breeds (retrievers, huskies) benefit from
brushing several times a week to control shedding and matting. Most dogs only
need a bath every one to three months unless they get dirty.

## Enrichment and training
tags: dog, enrichment, play, training, mental
Dogs need mental stimulation as well as physical exercise. Short daily training
sessions, food puzzles, and play prevent boredom and destructive behavior.
Ten to fifteen minutes of focused enrichment a day makes a noticeable difference,
especially for young or high-energy dogs.

## Health routine
tags: dog, health, vet, teeth, nails
Brush a dog's teeth several times a week to prevent dental disease. Trim nails
roughly every three to four weeks if they aren't worn down naturally. Keep up
with annual vet checkups and parasite prevention.

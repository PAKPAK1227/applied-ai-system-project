# Cat Care Guidelines

## Litter box care
tags: cat, litter, litter box, cleaning, hygiene
Scoop a cat's litter box at least once a day, and fully change the litter weekly.
Cats are sensitive to a dirty box and may stop using it if it isn't kept clean.
The common guideline is one box per cat plus one extra, placed in quiet locations.

## Feeding
tags: cat, feeding, food, meals, water
Adult cats are typically fed twice a day, or given controlled portions across the
day. Cats often drink too little, so wet food and multiple water sources help keep
them hydrated. Keep feeding on a consistent schedule and avoid free-feeding if a
cat tends to overeat.

## Play and enrichment
tags: cat, play, enrichment, exercise, hunting
Cats need short bursts of active play that mimic hunting — wand toys, chasing, and
climbing. Two or three play sessions of about 10-15 minutes a day keep indoor cats
active and reduce stress and destructive behavior.

## Grooming
tags: cat, grooming, brushing, coat, shedding
Most short-haired cats groom themselves and need only occasional brushing.
Long-haired cats (Persians, Maine Coons) need brushing most days to prevent
mats and reduce hairballs. Nail trims every couple of weeks help protect furniture
and paws.

## Health routine
tags: cat, health, vet, teeth
Keep up with annual vet visits and parasite prevention. Dental health matters for
cats too — dental treats and periodic checks help. Watch for changes in litter box
habits, appetite, or weight, which are often the first signs of illness.
